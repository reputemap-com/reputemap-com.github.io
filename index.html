ReputeMap for developers

ReputeMap manages Google reviews for multi-location businesses and agencies: alerts, AI reply drafts you approve, review requests by email or text, and reports for every location. Everything below is public and free to use.

Hosted MCP server for Google reviews

Connect Claude, Cursor or any MCP client to the Google reviews you manage. Endpoint: https://api.reputemap.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP). Tools: list_locations, list_reviews, get_review_stats, send_review_request, get_usage.

claude mcp add --transport http reputemap https://api.reputemap.com/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $REPUTEMAP_API_KEY"
